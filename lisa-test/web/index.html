UART console

Click the console to type. With the bring-up demo running, press ? for the banner. When the debugger owns the UART you are typing debugger commands (v, r02⏎, w020000⏎ …).

Elevator — 6 floors, 2 cars TTLC not running

Buttons are momentary: they are pressed while you hold them. The indicators (and the cars) come from the TTLC's outputs, refreshed live while it runs with the tick on. Hall calls on the left, each car's cabin panel beside its shaft. Floors are 1–6 here; the program and the I/O grid count them 0–5.
